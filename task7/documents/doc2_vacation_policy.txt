All full-time employees at Elevo receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly starting from the first day of employment. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the following calendar year, which must be utilized before March 31st. In addition to annual leave, Elevo provides 10 days of paid sick leave annually. Vacation requests exceeding three consecutive days require managerial approval at least two weeks in advance.
