Elevo operates under a flexible hybrid work model. Engineers may work remotely up to four days per week, with Wednesday designated as an in-office collaboration day. Core working hours for all team members across time zones are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time to facilitate synchronous meetings and pair programming. New team members receive a one-time remote home-office setup stipend of $500 to procure monitors, ergonomic chairs, and desk peripherals.
